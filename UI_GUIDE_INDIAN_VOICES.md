## 🖱️ How to Use:

### To Select Indian Male (Already Selected!):
```
✅ It's already selected by default!
✅ Just click "Generate Podcast"
✅ You'll get Indian accent automatically!
```

### To Select Indian Female:
```
1. Look for the orange/green highlighted section at the top
2. Click on "🇮🇳 Indian Female" card (right side)
3. Card will get orange border and checkmark
4. Click "Generate Podcast"
```

### To Use US Voices or Custom:
```
1. Click "▼ Other Voice Options (US Accent & Custom)"
2. Section expands showing all other options
3. Click any voice card you want
4. Selected voice gets purple border and checkmark
```
