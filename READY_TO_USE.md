## 💡 **Tips for Best Results**

### **Recording Your Voice:**
1. **Duration:** 10-30 seconds (optimal: 15 seconds)
2. **Content:** Read naturally, vary your tone
3. **Environment:** Quiet room, no echo
4. **Quality:** Clear recording, no distortion
5. **Format:** MP3 or WAV recommended

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting**

### **"Voice sample too short"**
- Upload at least 10 seconds
- Recommended: 15-20 seconds

### **"Model not loading"**
- First run downloads model (~1.2 GB)
- Wait for download to complete
- Requires ~2GB free disk space

### **"Slow generation"**
- Normal on CPU (2-3x real-time)
- For faster: Use GPU (see optimization guide)

### **"Poor quality"**
- Record in quiet environment
- Speak clearly and naturally
- Use good quality microphone
